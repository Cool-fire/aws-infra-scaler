# Integrations

aws-infra-scaler is designed to work alongside the tools and services already in your AWS workflow. It integrates with ECS, RDS, and Auto Scaling Groups out of the box, and exposes a simple configuration-driven interface that fits naturally into CI/CD pipelines, scheduled Lambda jobs, or manual operational runbooks. This document covers the supported integration patterns and how to wire them into your existing infrastructure.
